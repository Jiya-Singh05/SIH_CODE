Problem Statement 26133

One patient's journey — from symptom to cure, without the wait.

Rural and underserved communities often face long travel distances, fragmented records, and delayed referrals. Gram Arogya Connect follows Sunita Tai, a farm worker from a remote village, through triage, teleconsultation, appointments, referral, diagnostics, medicines, and follow-up — showing how every module closes a gap in the public-health chain.
